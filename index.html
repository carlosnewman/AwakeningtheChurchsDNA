These six e-books form a sequence of studies that follows the advance of the early Church and turns the events of Acts into applications for the Church today.

EBOOK 01

The Power That Transforms

Acts 1–2 — The outpouring of the Holy Spirit and its impact on the preaching and character of the first leaders.

EBOOK 02

A Living Community

Acts 2–4 — How the first community learned to live in fellowship, care for the needy, and center everything on Christ.

EBOOK 03

Faith That Endures

Acts 5–7 — The first internal and external challenges faced by the Church, and the integrity forged under pressure.

EBOOK 04

Mission Without Borders

Acts 8–9 — The gospel breaks through borders, from a desert road to the conversion of one of its greatest opponents.

EBOOK 05

Grace Without Limits

Acts 10–12 — God broadens the reach of His grace, culminating in a community sustained by persistent prayer.

EBOOK 06

Awakening the Church's DNA

Acts 29 — An invitation to a practical revival today, living out the same Gospel that transformed that generation.
